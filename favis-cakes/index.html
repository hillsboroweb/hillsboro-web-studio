Before your celebration

A few good questions.

How do I start a custom cake inquiry?

Call (541) 980-9582 or visit the bakery’s Instagram profile. Have your celebration date, estimated servings, and design ideas ready. You can use the “Plan your cake” button to prepare a note for that conversation.

What flavors and desserts are available?

Customer accounts mention cakes, cupcakes, cheesecake, and other desserts, including churro and tiramisu flavors. These are past experiences, rather than a current menu. Ask the bakery which choices are available for your date.

How much will a cake cost, and how far ahead should I order?

Ask the bakery for a quote and current lead time. Share the size, design, occasion, and date before agreeing on an order. Pricing and booking policies have not been confirmed for this concept page.

Can I ask about ingredients or allergies?

Yes—contact the bakery directly before ordering to discuss ingredients, dietary needs, and cross-contact. This page does not confirm any allergen-free options.
